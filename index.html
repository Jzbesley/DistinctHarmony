Meet Your Consultant

A decade of connecting vision with resources.

Candace Scroggins is a strategic philanthropy and community development leader with more than a decade of experience helping nonprofit organizations, community development corporations, healthcare providers, educational institutions, and small businesses secure funding, strengthen organizational capacity, and create lasting community impact.

As Founder and Principal Consultant of Distinct Harmony Consulting, Candace has secured more than $10 million in federal, state, corporate, and foundation funding to support initiatives focused on family stability, affordable housing, behavioral health, youth development, economic mobility, community revitalization, and health equity. She partners with organizations to develop comprehensive fundraising strategies, cultivate philanthropic partnerships, design innovative community programs, and implement sustainable solutions that address complex social challenges.
